Endless Inventory: Refined Integration
======================================

A short list of what this mod adds. For the whole thing, see README.md; for what was merged and
changed, see ChangeLog.md.


2.0.0
-----

Refined Storage

 - A portable grid with no storage disk opens on the player's Endless Inventory instead of
   refusing to work. A grid with a disk behaves as it did.
 - A whole network can draw on it too: an External Storage bus pointed at a Security Manager
   offers the Endless Inventory of the first player that manager gives every permission to.
   The bus is what puts the manager on the network, so nothing else has to be placed.
 - The Endless Inventory panel is kept off the grid screen, which shows the same contents.

Endless Inventory as inventory space

 - Items picked up into the part of the inventory the hotbar is not showing are swept into the
   Endless Inventory. Only passively gained items: anything moved around inside a GUI is left
   alone, so shift-clicking cannot be undone behind the player's back.
 - Items that fit nowhere go to the Endless Inventory rather than onto the ground.
 - Slots a hotbar row is showing are never touched by either.

Items keep what they carry

 - Enchantments, custom names, potion contents and Forge capability data survive both into the
   store and on the way to the screen. Previously the tag was dropped on the wire and capability
   data was never carried at all.

Multi-group hotbar

 - The nine slots become up to four groups of nine, selectable across the thirty-six inventory
   slots.
 - Ctrl+1..9 show pages of a table of eighty-one Endless Inventory items.
 - Ctrl+0 swaps the hotbar between the player's own inventory and their ender chest. A death or a
   logout puts the ender chest's contents back first.
 - A layout key cycles how the groups are drawn, and a nine by nine panel over container screens
   shows the whole table: right-drag to move it, shift-click a cell to take an item off the hotbar.
 - Not compatible with the standalone QuadHotbar mod, which patches the same instruction. When it
   is present the hotbar switches itself off rather than failing to start.

A panel you can move

 - The Endless Inventory panel beside a container screen can be dragged by its frame, and comes
   back where it was left.

Fixes

 - A dedicated server no longer crashes when the panel is opened.
 - Closing the crafter no longer hands over the crafted result along with the ingredients.
 - A recipe transfer no longer leaves the client drawing a crafter the server does not have.
 - Clicking an item no longer destroys it, and shift-clicking no longer duplicates it.
 - Moving items quickly no longer sends the whole inventory over the network each time.
