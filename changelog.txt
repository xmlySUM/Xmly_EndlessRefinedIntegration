Endless Refined changelog
=========================

1.0.0
-----
First release.

Refined Storage
 - A portable grid with no storage disk opens, backed by the player's Endless Inventory.
   Disk behaviour is untouched when a disk is present.
 - Endless Inventory's attached overlay is kept off the grid screen, which shows the same
   contents.

Endless Inventory
 - Passively gained items that land in slots 9-35 are moved to Endless Inventory. Items
   moved around inside a GUI are not, so shift-clicking cannot be undone behind the
   player's back.
 - Items that fit nowhere go to Endless Inventory instead of onto the ground.
 - Item NBT now survives the trip to the client: three points in Endless Inventory's own
   encoding dropped it, so icons of enchanted, potioned and other NBT-bearing items were
   drawn as plain items.

Hotbar
 - Up to four groups of nine slots, selectable across the extended inventory slots.
 - Ctrl+1..9 show pages of a table of eighty-one Endless Inventory items.
 - Ctrl+0 swaps between the player's own inventory and their ender chest, with the ender
   chest's contents moved back before a death or a logout.
 - The grave key cycles the layout: one row of groups, two, or one row per group.
 - A nine by nine panel over container screens, with right-drag to move, shift-click to
   clear a cell, and shift-click in the Endless Inventory panel to fill one.

Forked from QuadHotbar
 - The multi-group hotbar is a fork of QuadHotbar, which the two mods cannot share; see
   THIRD-PARTY.md. Installing both is refused with an explanation.
